As $T_f$ increases from 1 to 256, the number of exact mapping calls per commodity decreases by 98.9\% on Milan and 98.5\% on the Random topology, while the amortized mapping computation time per slot decreases by 98.8\% and 98.1\%, respectively.

Mapping time covers the complete mapping call, including bridge serialization, process startup, solving, and result parsing. The measurements were collected with up to 75 concurrent experiment processes on a 64-core host and include host contention; they are not isolated solver CPU times.
